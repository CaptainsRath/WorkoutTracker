## Project Summary

Our current idea is to make Tinder for cats. The website will function similarly to Tinder by providing possible matches for the cats to swipe on. The matches will be provided based on metrics gathered by the website such as left-right swipe ratio, common interests, etc.

We have two additional backup ideas that we have brainstormed: a website which will suggest recipes to cook given a grocery list and a workout generator which will suggest workouts based on previously completed exercises and the interests of the user.
